# Executive Summary

Combat Connect is a cloud-based Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform designed to help martial arts organizations manage their daily operations through one unified platform.

The platform replaces manual processes, spreadsheets, and disconnected applications with a single modern system.

---

# Vision

To become the world's leading management platform for martial arts organizations.

---

# Mission

Provide every martial arts organization with an affordable, scalable, and professional operating system that simplifies administration and improves the experience of owners, instructors, students, and parents.

---



# Target Customers

Combat Connect is designed for:

- Independent Dojos
- Martial Arts Academies
- Multi-Branch Organizations
- National Associations
- Tournament Organizers

Supported martial arts include:

- Taekwondo
- Karate
- Judo
- Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu
- Muay Thai
- Boxing
- Wrestling
- MMA
- Other combat sports

# Primary Users



## Organization Owner

Responsible for managing the entire organization.

Responsibilities:

- Organization Management
- Billing
- Staff Management
- Reports
- Subscription Management

---



## Master

Responsible for technical operations.

Responsibilities:

- Student Promotions
- Belt Examinations
- Certificates
- Class Management
- Attendance
- Student Evaluation

---



## Instructor

Responsible for teaching.

Responsibilities:

- Take Attendance
- View Assigned Classes
- View Students
- Record Class Notes

Instructors cannot promote students unless they also have the Master role.

---



## Student

Can:

- View Profile
- Attendance History
- Rank
- Certificates
- Payment Status
- Announcements

---



## Parent

Can:

- View Child Progress
- Attendance
- Payments
- Announcements
- Certificates

---



# Role Philosophy

One user may have multiple roles.

Examples:

- Owner + Master
- Master + Instructor
- Owner + Instructor

A user always logs in with a single account.

Permissions are determined by assigned roles.

# Success Metrics

Combat Connect will be considered successful when:

- Organizations can operate entirely within the platform.
- Manual paperwork is significantly reduced.
- Student management becomes faster and more accurate.
- Owners gain visibility through reports and dashboards.
- The platform scales to support thousands of organizations.

---



# Product Principles

The platform should always be:

- Simple
- Fast
- Reliable
- Secure
- Scalable
- Mobile Friendly
- Offline Ready
- Easy to Learn

---



# Out of Scope

Combat Connect is not intended to become:

- A Social Media Platform
- A Learning Management System
- A Video Streaming Platform
- A General School Information System

The focus remains on operating martial arts organizations.
